EduFOSSpage is a small, privacy-respecting client for the EduPage school
information system. It shows your timetable, grades and messages in a clean,
distraction-free interface.

Features
* Timetable by day
* Grades by subject and term, with weighted averages
* Messages with a read-only detail view
* Credentials stored encrypted on the device

Privacy
* No analytics, no ads, no WebView, no Google Play Services
* Talks only to your school's EduPage server

There is also a companion Wear OS build for supported watches.
